# Scope questions for the business owner

**For:** Iris Poesse (Head of PRP) · **Prepared:** 15 Oct 2026 · **Status:** to send. Record the answers in `docs/open-decisions.md` (PS-1 to PS-6) and in a meeting record.

The team asked for "MIP and EDT together, hosted with SSO". There are three more tools in the same area (see `docs/tool-landscape.md`). These questions fix the scope and the finish line.

## Questions

1. **Tools in scope.** Besides MIP Console and EDT, which tools should be part of this? Known so far: PRPL Portfolio Insights, Aria 2 / PeerPulse, the PRP tools hub, and Gayatri's Excel macro. Is there anything else PRPLs or EAs use?
2. **Users for release 1.** PRPLs only, or vendor EAs too? Do EAs have Wiley (Entra) accounts?
3. **Must-haves for release 1.** What are the 3–5 things it must do on day one? Everything else goes to a later release.
4. **Decisions.** Is Iris the final approver for rules and email wording?
5. **Success.** What should improve, and can we measure where it stands today?
6. **Ownership.** Who owns and supports the tool after launch? When do the old copies get switched off?
7. **PRPL Portfolio Insights.** Who built it, and who uses it? Should it join the same hosted site as a PRPL oversight view, or stay separate?
8. **PeerPulse / MIRU.** How does Aria relate to this work: merge, replace, or a separate product that we link to?
9. **AI features.** Portfolio Insights sends manuscript IDs and editor names to an AI model. Are its AI summaries a must-have? If so, they need an approved Wiley AI service.
10. **Which "overdue" is the standard?** Health Check (Portfolio Insights, Aria) or "needs action" (the console)? The dashboards and the EA reminders should tell the same story.

## Proposed finish line (for Iris to react to)

- **Release 1:** one hosted, SSO-protected tool (v2.2: MIP + EDT), used by PRPLs and one pilot vendor's EAs. The open rules are signed off, UAT is passed, the old copies are retired, and a support owner is named.
- **Release 2:** Portfolio Insights folded in as a PRPL oversight view (v2.3), once questions 7, 9 and 10 are answered.
- **Separate:** Aria / PeerPulse stays the managers' analytics product, linked from the tool.
