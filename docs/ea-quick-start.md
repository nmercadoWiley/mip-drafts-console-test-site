# EA quick start: editor reminders with MIP Drafts Console

**For:** editorial assistants · **Tool version:** v2.2 · **Status: DRAFT.** Items marked ⚠ are waiting on PRP decisions. Don't hand this to EAs until they're filled in.

Follow these steps exactly. **Don't change settings unless your PRPL tells you to.**

## Before you start

- You need **Chrome or Edge**, and the **latest MIP Drafts Console file** from ⚠ *[where PRP publishes it: tools hub / link]*.
- Check the footer at the bottom of the tool. It should say **templates v1.0.0** ⚠ *(or the current approved version)*. If it shows an older version, get the new file before you send anything.
- You need the journal's **MIP report**. It arrives by email in the journal's editorial office inbox.

## When to send

⚠ *[Per journal, agreed with the editors. For example: "JDE: the 15th of each month", "HEPR: every Friday". PRP to supply the list.]*

## Steps

1. **Open the report email** in the editorial office inbox and save the attached Excel file.
2. **Open the tool** by double-clicking `mip-drafts-console.html`. Stay on the first tab, **Editor, Triage & EA Drafts**.
3. **Step 01: drop the Excel file** onto the first box, "MIP Summary Report".
   - ✅ You should see something like "✓ file name — 85 manuscript rows".
   - ❌ If a yellow **Heads up** box appears, you have the wrong file. Stop and tell your PRPL.
4. **Step 03: leave every setting as it is.** Only fill in:
   - **EA email address:** the journal's editorial office address.
   - **Sign-off name:** ⚠ *[the journal office name, or your name, per PRP]*.
5. Click **Generate all drafts**.
6. **Step 04: check the last tile says "Accounted for X / X"**, with both numbers the same. If they're different, stop and tell your PRPL.
7. **Step 06, Handling Editor drafts:** click an editor's name to open their draft.
   - Read it quickly. Do the manuscripts look right? Has anything already been dealt with since the report was produced?
   - Send it in **one** of these ways:
     - **Outlook:** click **Download .eml**, open the file, check the **To:** line, then click Send.
     - **Freshdesk / other:** click **Copy HTML**, paste it into the reply, check the recipient, then send. If formatting is lost, use **Copy plain text** instead.
   - Repeat for each editor.
8. **Step 07, Triage Editor drafts:** do the same.
9. **Step 12: click Download draft status export (.xlsx)** and save it in ⚠ *[the agreed folder]*. This is your record of what was drafted and why.
10. **Close the tab.** The tool doesn't keep any data.

⚠ *This needs the Reviewer Performance Report, which EAs can't get yet (dependency D2). Leave these steps out until access is in place.*

## When to contact your PRPL

- The wrong file was loaded, or a "Heads up" warning appears.
- The "Accounted for" numbers don't match.
- A draft goes to the wrong person, or lists a paper you know is finished.
- An editor hasn't responded to **⚠ [N]** reminders in a row. Your PRPL will escalate it.

## Don'ts

- Don't change thresholds or settings. The PRP standard is already set.
- Don't forward the tool to colleagues. New EAs get it through ⚠ *[vendor management]*.
- Don't save exports or drafts anywhere except ⚠ *[the agreed location]*. They contain author and editor names.
